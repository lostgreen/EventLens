Abstract

Recent multimodal large language models achieve strong results on video question answering, yet often fail to recover the temporal structure that makes a video coherent. They may merge neighboring events, miss single-event progression, or infer implausible relations across events. We study structured temporal understanding as the ability to recover event structure from visual evidence, including event boundaries, single-event progression, and multi-event relations.

We propose EventLens, a vision-centric reinforcement learning framework that learns video understanding through event-structure recovery. Under controlled perturbations of temporal granularity, playback direction, and event order, models learn to recover the underlying structure. Three verifiable task families enable task-specialized GRPO training with deterministic rewards. Multi-teacher on-policy distillation (MT-OPD) then consolidates the specialized policies into a unified model. Experiments on Qwen3-VL backbones show consistent improvements on temporal grounding and event-sensitive reasoning while preserving general video-QA performance.
